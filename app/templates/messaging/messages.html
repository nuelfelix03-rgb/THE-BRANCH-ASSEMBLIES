Send a message to your active members or to those celebrating birthdays.

For SMS, only the first 160 characters are delivered.
Setup notes
  • Email uses the SMTP account configured in .env (MAIL_* variables). Set MAIL_USERNAME, MAIL_PASSWORD, and MAIL_DEFAULT_SENDER to enable sending.
  • SMS is sent through an SMS gateway via SMS_API_URL and SMS_API_KEY in .env. Until configured, SMS is logged to the console instead of being sent.
